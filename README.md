# 108 NAMA SHIVA

A quiet digital manuscript for the 108 names of Shiva — a reading sanctuary to be opened slowly, one name at a time.

This is the Aṣṭottara Śata Nāmāvalī presented as a calm, unhurried reading experience. Each name stands on its own page, with its source meaning alongside it. Nothing hurries you to move on. The work is made to be read at a human pace, in silence if you like.

The intention behind it is simple:

- to make the 108 names accessible in a quiet, digital form
- to preserve the source meaning as it was written
- to give each name space to be read slowly and sat with
- to accompany the text with subtle, symbolic artwork where available
- to keep the whole experience free from distraction

## Tentang

The manuscript contains 108 names. Each one carries a meaning that has been passed down through tradition — read here as a sequence, a journey from one name to the next.

The content is based on the source manuscript *108 Nama Dewa Siwa*. The original wording and transcription have been kept as they are, including their imperfections. Nothing has been corrected or polished beyond the source, out of respect for where the text comes from.

Number 7 is not present in the source manuscript. It is left as a silent, empty folio — not filled in, not invented. Its absence is part of the manuscript as it was found.

## Sebuah Ruang untuk Membaca

Buka satu nama. Baca maknanya. Duduk sejenak dengannya sebelum melanjutkan ke yang berikutnya.

Tidak ada urutan yang harus dikejar. Anda bisa mulai dari awal, membuka halaman mana saja dari daftar, atau kembali ke nama yang ingin Anda baca lagi. Setiap folio berdiri sendiri, dan juga bagian dari keseluruhan.

## Bahasa

The reading interface is available in Bahasa Indonesia and English. You can switch between the two at any time — the names themselves remain as in the source, while the surrounding text follows the language you choose.
